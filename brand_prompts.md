# Identidad corporativa de Cuidar Conecta

## Dirección de marca

Cuidar Conecta presenta a **Marcia Barbosa** como una figura de referencia que une conocimiento, escucha y organización del cuidado. La voz es cálida, precisa y respetuosa: reconoce la complejidad cotidiana de los equipos y las familias sin recurrir a urgencia artificial, promesas de resultados ni lenguaje clínico prescriptivo.

La expresión visual combina una base marfil cálida, azul petróleo profundo, verde salvia y acentos coral apagado. La tipografía sugerida contrapone una serif editorial de alta legibilidad para titulares —DM Serif Display— y una sans humanista contemporánea para información operativa —Manrope—. El resultado debe comunicar tranquilidad, criterio y cercanía profesional.

| Elemento | Decisión |
| --- | --- |
| Personalidad | Serena, humana, rigurosa y acogedora. |
| Promesa | Convertir la conversación sobre cuidado en experiencias formativas claras y respetuosas. |
| Audiencia principal | Direcciones, coordinaciones y equipos de centros de cuidado; organizaciones comunitarias y redes de atención. |
| Tono | Hablar con las instituciones, no desde encima de ellas. Usar «acompañar», «conversar», «fortalecer» y «adaptar al contexto». |
| Límites | No diagnosticar, no indicar tratamientos, no asegurar resultados, no sustituir a los equipos asistenciales. |

## Cinco prompts creativos

### 1. Imagen hero editorial: referencia cercana

```text
Create an editorial hero photograph for the institutional website of Cuidar Conecta, led by Marcia Barbosa, for directors and teams at care centers.
Subject: a warm, confident Brazilian woman in her late 40s or early 50s, professional yet approachable, in a quiet conversation with a small multidisciplinary care team in a bright, contemporary care-center lounge; no medical procedure, no patient vulnerability, no uniforms with visible logos.
Composition: wide 16:9 landscape, subject placed on the right third, generous clean negative space on the left for website headline, candid attentive gestures, a small notebook and natural plants in the background.
Style: premium editorial photography, warm daylight, ivory walls, deep petrol blue, sage green and muted coral details, natural textures, quiet confidence, realistic human expressions.
Text/content to render: no text.
Constraints: polished but non-stock-photo, inclusive adults, privacy-respecting scene, web hero safe area on the left.
Avoid: clinical procedures, hospital drama, stethoscopes, overly posed corporate smiles, miracle imagery, visible brand logos, text or watermarks.
```

### 2. Campaña de charla: conversación que organiza el cuidado

```text
Create a vertical 4:5 social-media campaign visual for Cuidar Conecta promoting an institutional talk by Marcia Barbosa.
Subject: an intimate semi-circle learning session in a care center, with Marcia Barbosa facilitating a thoughtful conversation among caregivers and coordinators, an abstract wall print of interconnected lines in the background.
Composition: balanced central group, strong eye contact and listening gestures, clear calm area in the upper third for a short headline, visual hierarchy designed for LinkedIn and Instagram.
Style: refined editorial documentary photography, soft natural light, ivory, petrol blue, sage and muted coral palette, high-end nonprofit and wellbeing brand aesthetic.
Text/content to render: "Cuidar también se conversa"; secondary line: "Charlas para equipos y centros de cuidado"; signature: "Marcia Barbosa · Cuidar Conecta".
Constraints: Spanish text exactly as written, legible and minimal, realistic adult people, no patient-identifying details.
Avoid: crowded layouts, generic conference stages, medical claims, excessive visual effects, distorted hands, logos or watermarks.
```

### 3. Formación institucional: aprendizaje práctico y respetuoso

```text
Create a horizontal 16:9 brand image for a Cuidar Conecta webpage section about training for care centers.
Subject: a table-level view of a facilitated workshop with diverse adult care professionals reviewing printed discussion cards, writing shared priorities and exchanging ideas; Marcia Barbosa is present as a guide, not as an authority figure above the group.
Composition: hands, cards and attentive faces in a warm meeting room; leave spacious negative area on the right for section copy; show collaboration, not instruction.
Style: premium magazine photography, tactile paper, wooden table, soft window light, understated petrol blue and sage elements, natural beige palette.
Text/content to render: no text.
Constraints: all materials generic and unreadable, no medical records, no clinical directions, privacy-safe and respectful.
Avoid: treatment demonstrations, patient scenes, prescriptive medical imagery, desk clutter, visible institutional trademarks, watermarks.
```

### 4. Sistema gráfico: red de cuidado y dignidad

```text

Subject: a subtle network of rounded connection lines, open circles, leaf-like curves and gentle concentric waves, symbolizing listening, continuity, community and human dignity.
Composition: repeatable square tile with balanced breathing space; use as a light website background and presentation divider.
Style: sophisticated minimal vector illustration, hand-drawn precision, editorial health-and-wellbeing identity; warm ivory base, delicate sage and pale petrol lines, a few muted coral focal points.
Text/content to render: no text.
Constraints: flat vector, elegant at both small and large sizes, low visual noise, no gradients required.
Avoid: crosses, hearts, medical icons, literal hands, puzzle pieces, childish motifs, lettering, logos or watermarks.
```

